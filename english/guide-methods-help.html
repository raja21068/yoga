Techniques Are Helpful

Methods can save much wandering, but a method itself is not meditation. Its purpose is to bring you toward witnessing.

Read the Book1. Methods and Meditation

What this chapter is saying

The book affirms the value of techniques: methods shaped by long practice can save the meditator from many unproductive detours. But it immediately adds another layer—real meditation is not a technique; it is a quality of awareness.

The relationship in the book

Think of technique as a bridge across a gap. At first it helps you move from habitual mind toward awareness. One day, when witnessing is happening naturally, the bridge has completed its work.

What it means for practice

So neither dismiss methods nor make them sacred. Use them carefully in the beginning, while remembering that breathing, shaking, dancing, sound, and posture are doorways. Witnessing is the direction.

Remember While Using a Method
  1. First follow the method in the structure in which it is given.
  2. During the practice, notice whether there is a moment when doing continues and you also clearly see that the doing is happening.
  3. Do not chase that moment; let the method do its work.
