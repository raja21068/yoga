The Goose Has Never Been Inside!

You are neither the bright side nor the dark side of the mind. The real difficulty comes from identification, not from figuring out how to get the 'goose' out of the bottle.

Read the Book2. The Goose Has Never Been Inside!

What is the question?

The questioner is frightened by the dark side of the mind and feels shame and guilt, while also wanting to accept every aspect of the mind.

The koan in the book

A master asks a disciple to solve the puzzle: a goose has grown inside a bottle; how can it be taken out without breaking the bottle or killing the goose? The disciple finally sees it: the bottle symbolizes the mind, the goose awareness. The answer is—the goose was never in the bottle.

What it means for practice

Light and dark are two poles of the mind. If you identify with the attractive pole, the rejected pole will still hold you. The book proposes choiceless watching: step back a little and watch the mind like a film without making any scene into 'me.'

Do Not Solve an Imprisonment That Does Not Exist
  1. When an 'unwanted me' appears, do not rush to drive it away.
  2. Do not cling to the 'good side' as proof of who you are either.
  3. Ask: if all of this can be seen, is the awareness seeing it really imprisoned inside it?
