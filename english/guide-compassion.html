Compassion

In the language of the book, compassion is the quality that appears when love and meditation meet.

Read the Book2. Meditation in Flowering

What this chapter is saying

The book describes compassion as love that is no longer only desire or need and no longer waits for a return, but begins to become sharing. Meditation enters it so that sharing comes not only from emotion but with awareness.

The contrast in the book

The book contrasts a beggar and an emperor: beggar-like love asks, 'Give me'; abundant love gives because there is already something overflowing within. The image points to a different inner source.

What it means for practice

This card is not asking you to act kind. It helps you notice whether giving secretly calculates a return. If there is calculation, first see it. Witnessing is more important than pretending to be selfless.

Reading Reminder
  1. Do not force yourself to become a 'compassionate person.'
  2. First see clearly the relationship between need, expectation, and giving; clarity itself is part of meditation.
