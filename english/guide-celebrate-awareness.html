Celebrate Awareness

When genuine silence appears after catharsis, do not doubt it because 'things are going too well.' The book asks you to nourish the moment through celebration, creativity, and sharing.

Read the Book7. Celebrate Awareness

What is the question?

After years of cathartic meditation, the questioner feels inner harmony and centering, but worries because he has heard that a great chaos must come before samadhi. He wonders whether he has missed the 'right' stage.

The turn in the answer

The book explains that the very purpose of dynamic and cathartic methods is to release chaos within the method. When silence comes, there is no need to manufacture another chaos to fit an idea. Doubt itself can become the seed of a new disturbance.

What it means for practice

When silence, balance, or joy genuinely appears, live it first. Celebrate it, share it, let it enter dance, song, or creativity. The next moment grows from this one; do not destroy the silence already here for the sake of a future idea of the 'correct process.'

Water the Silence
  1. When a clear, quiet moment appears today, do not immediately interrogate whether it is real.
  2. Celebrate it with one simple action: walk, write one sentence, make one brushstroke, dance for a while.
  3. Do not manufacture the next stage; let the next moment grow naturally from this one.
