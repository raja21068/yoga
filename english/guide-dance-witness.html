Only Witnessing Can Truly Dance

The question is not whether witnessing turns life into observation from the sidelines, but whether you can participate totally while remaining awake within.

Read the Book1. Only Witnessing Can Truly Dance

What is the question?

The questioner worries that if he keeps witnessing, he may only stand beside life watching other people sing, dance, and love while losing his own participation.

The turn in the answer

The book distinguishes a spectator from a witness. A spectator withdraws and becomes numb; a witness can live more intensely and passionately while remembering, deep within action, that he is awareness. The image is a lotus in water yet untouched by the water.

What it means for practice

Witnessing is not escape from action; it changes mechanical reaction into aware action. When someone pushes your 'button,' you do not have to react automatically. From present awareness you can act, remain silent, sing, or dance.

Participate Without Falling Asleep
  1. Choose an activity you genuinely enjoy today: walking, cooking, dancing, or working.
  2. Do it wholeheartedly without keeping part of your energy aside to judge how well you are performing.
  3. At the same time leave a little wakefulness: the body is moving, things are happening, and you know they are happening.
