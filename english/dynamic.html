Dynamic Meditation

Through chaotic breathing, catharsis, the Hoo sound, stillness, and celebration, take doing to its peak and then enter non-doing and witnessing.

60 minCore: Witnessing
How do I begin this meditation?

For your first session, watch the video to understand the movements and stages. For the actual meditation, play the practice audio, put the phone aside, and follow the full process. Download the audio first if you want to practice offline.

Practice style

Read in order: first understand the instruction for this stage, then look at the matching movement image. During the actual meditation, you can play the practice audio below.

Preparation

Prepare

Find a quiet, private space. Wear comfortable clothing, remove distractions, and make sure you have room to move freely.

Step 1

Chaotic breathing (10 minutes)

For about 10 minutes, breathe rapidly, deeply, and without a fixed rhythm while keeping the body relaxed.

Step 2

Catharsis (10 minutes)

For about 10 minutes, let suppressed energy express itself freely through sound and movement.

Step 3

Jumping with Hoo (10 minutes)

For about 10 minutes, raise your arms and jump, throwing yourself totally into the sound 'Hoo'.

Step 4

Stop (15 minutes)

Stop completely. Stay in the position you are in and simply witness whatever is happening.

Step 5

Celebration (15 minutes)

Celebrate with music and dance for about 15 minutes.

Do Not Lose the Witness
The method is only an entrance. Whether it uses breath, dance, sound, darkness, sitting, or relationship, what matters most is gradually seeing what is happening rather than only chasing an experience.
Body Safety Note (website addition)
Put comfort and safety first. Stop if you experience significant dizziness, pain, difficulty breathing, or loss of balance. Intense breathing, whirling, or breath-retention practices are not suitable for everyone.
