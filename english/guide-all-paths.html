All Paths Lead to the Summit

The book does not rank awareness above love or love above awareness. In the valley they seem like different paths; at the summit they are one whole.

Read the Book6. All Paths Lead to the Summit

What is the question?

The question asks whether awareness is a higher quality than love, assuming the two remain separate even at the end.

The turn in the answer

The book says that at the outer edges of life, truth, love, awareness, authenticity, and wholeness appear different; the closer you move toward the center, the more they converge. The path of awareness and the path of love can begin from different directions and meet at the same summit.

A way to check the direction

On the path of awareness, you can ask whether love is growing. On the path of love, you can ask whether awareness is appearing. If awareness becomes colder and loveless, or love becomes possessive and unconscious, it has not yet become pure.

Do Not Argue About Which Path Is Higher
  1. Notice which doorway you mainly enter through: awareness, love, devotion, or action.
  2. Do not use that choice to dismiss another path.
  3. Let the paths check each other: does awareness make love more natural, and does love make awareness clearer?
