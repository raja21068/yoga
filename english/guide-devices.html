Devices Can Help, but They Cannot Create Meditation

Music, devices, and technology can help relaxation, but they cannot produce awareness for you.

Read the Book3. Guidelines to Freedom

What this chapter is saying

The book discusses the possibility of using brainwaves, sound, or devices to produce relaxation. It accepts that such tools may lead more quickly into a quiet, deep-sleep-like state, but calling that state meditation confuses two different things.

The boundary in the book

The real question is not how quiet the mind becomes, but whether awareness is still present in deep quiet. A device can create conditions; it cannot replace the small flame of awareness that remains awake.

What it means for practice

The same applies to the music and guided audio on this website: audio is support, not meditation itself. It can guide you through stages, but witnessing cannot be downloaded and a player cannot do it for you.

When Using Media
  1. Let the audio carry the timing and stages; do not keep looking at the phone.
  2. When things become quiet, notice whether clear awareness is still present.
  3. When one day you can enter without relying on audio, recognize that the tool has completed part of its work.
