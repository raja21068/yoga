Meditation Is a Knack

Meditation has a scientific side and an artistic side, but the book finally prefers to call it a 'knack.'

Read the Book3. Guidelines to Freedom

What this chapter is saying

It is like science because methods have clear structures; it is like art because it is closer to music, poetry, dance, and the heart. Yet neither word fully captures meditation, because the essential point cannot be transferred as information alone.

The story in the book

The book uses learning to swim as an image. Someone can explain the movements, but nobody can discover buoyancy in the water for you. The moment you really learn to swim is when the body suddenly finds harmony with the water. It is not more theory; it is a knack.

What it means for practice

Reading the website matters, but reading cannot replace practice. Once the instructions are understood, actually enter the method. The knack is usually not found by reading more, but by entering the practice again and again until it happens by itself.

From Reading to Practice
  1. Understand one method.
  2. Choose a safe and suitable time to actually practice it.
  3. Give yourself several chances; do not demand that you 'get it' the first time.
