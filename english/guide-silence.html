The Great Silence

Silence is not merely the absence of sound outside; it is an inner space that is not disturbed by changes around it.

Read the Book2. Meditation in Flowering

What this chapter is saying

The book describes silence as a positive presence, not as 'nothing.' Day and night, age, change, and sound continue outside, but the inner center is not shaken by them. Silence is less something you possess than something you discover yourself already within.

The image in the book

The book uses the center of a cyclone: the outer edge can move at great speed while the center remains still. The point is not to stop the outer movement, but to discover the center that does not rotate with it.

What it means for practice

Practicing silence does not mean lowering every sound, suppressing thought, or turning life blank. First allow the outer world to happen, then see whether there is an inner space that does not need to run after every sound and thought.

Reading Reminder
  1. When the environment is noisy, do not immediately treat the noise as failure.
  2. Hear the sound first, then notice whether, while sound is being heard, there is also a little space within.
