Playfulness

Turning meditation into a heavy task closes the mind. The book instead emphasizes play, celebration, and relaxation.

Read the Book3. Guidelines to Freedom

What this chapter is saying

This section directly challenges the image of meditation as grim seriousness. A real meditator is not someone who always wears a long face, but someone who can participate in life more playfully.

The word in the book

The book uses leela—life as play. Play does not mean carelessness; it means total involvement without turning every step into self-judgment.

What it means for practice

If practice repeatedly becomes 'Did I do well today? Am I progressing?' seriousness puts ego back at the center. Playfulness makes it easier to try, fail, and begin again without adding unnecessary weight.

Practice Reminder
  1. When choosing a method today, bring a little less 'I must succeed.'
  2. Do it completely, but allow curiosity to remain.
  3. Afterward, feel first; do not rush to grade the session.
