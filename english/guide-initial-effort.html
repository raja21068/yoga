Effort in the Beginning

In the beginning there is doing, but as a method matures, effort must gradually give way to non-doing.

Read the Book1. Methods and Meditation

What this chapter is saying

The book accepts an apparent paradox: meditation is ultimately effortless, yet beginners often have to start with effort. Even 'just sitting' can initially become an attempt.

The turn in the book

When a technique really works, effort becomes more and more natural, like breathing, until the sense of 'doing meditation' fades and only being and awareness remain. Effort is the beginning, not the goal.

What it means for practice

Feeling some effort does not mean you are failing. But if the practice becomes increasingly tense and controlling, notice when effort has changed from help into obstacle. The direction is from deliberate practice toward natural happening.

Practice Reminder
  1. At first, do the method completely; do not pretend you are already effortless.
  2. At the end, leave a little time to do nothing and let effort stop by itself.
