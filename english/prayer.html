Prayer Meditation

Use movements toward the sky and then toward the earth to experience receiving, giving, and merging; allow the body's trembling to happen naturally.

Night or Early MorningCore: Witnessing
How do I begin this meditation?

Read through the steps once first. During the actual meditation, play the practice audio; if you will practice without internet, download it to your phone beforehand.

▶ Play Practice Audio↓ Download Audio

Practice style

  1. Raise both hands toward the sky, palms upward, and feel energy entering.
  2. Allow gentle trembling to spread through the body without controlling it.
  3. Then bow toward the earth and touch or kiss the ground; the book suggests repeating the cycle several times and resting afterward.
Do Not Lose the Witness
The method is only an entrance. Whether it uses breath, dance, sound, darkness, sitting, or relationship, what matters most is gradually seeing what is happening rather than only chasing an experience.
