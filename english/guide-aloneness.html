Aloneness: Your Self-Nature

In the book, aloneness does not mean isolation. It means being able to find joy in your own inner space.

Read the Book2. Meditation in Flowering

What this chapter is saying

The inner journey can finally be entered only by you. The book is not rejecting relationship; it is pointing out that nobody else can enter your deepest center for you or keep it alive on your behalf.

The image in the book

Aloneness is compared to a lotus opening in the heart, and to the spontaneous song of a distant bird. The song is not trying to capture a particular listener; it comes because there is already so much within that it naturally overflows.

What it means for practice

If you can settle only when someone is with you, approves of you, or provides the right conditions, your center is still outside. The practice of aloneness is to discover that your own inner space can also be complete.

Try This Today
  1. Give yourself ten minutes without a phone, conversation, or task.
  2. Do not endure loneliness; simply see what happens inside when external stimulation is absent.
  3. If discomfort comes, include that discomfort too.
