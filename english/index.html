Do not treat this as a list where more is better. Choose one method intuitively and experience it yourself. If it feels intimate and right for you, stay with it and go deeper. The direction of every method is the same: awareness, witnessing, and returning to here and now.

01 · Choose IntuitivelyNotice which method attracts you in this moment.
02 · Play for Three DaysDo not become too serious; experience the same method completely.
03 · Go DeeperIf it clicks with you, stop switching so often and continue more deeply.

Part Four · Obstacles to Meditation

Follow the book’s three chapters: The Two Difficulties → Wrong Methods → The Tricks of the Mind. Each card addresses one specific obstacle.

Open

1. The Two Difficulties

The book names two major difficulties on the way to meditation: ego, and the mind that continually translates existence into words.

Reading

The Ego

Whether inflated or wounded, the ego turns meditation into the question 'Can I succeed?' The book asks you to see this constructed center.

Read this card
Reading

The Chattering Mind

The second great difficulty is not simply that thoughts exist, but that the mind instantly translates every experience into language, putting words between existence and awareness.

Read this card

2. Wrong Methods

Some practices look like meditation but lead toward tension or analysis. Here the book explains why concentration and introspection are not witnessing.

Reading

Meditation Is Not Concentration

Concentration narrows awareness to one point; meditation opens toward the whole. One creates tension, while the other begins in relaxation and acceptance.

Read this card
Reading

Meditation Is Not Introspection

Introspection analyzes 'why am I angry?' Witnessing directly sees the anger itself—without explanation, judgment, or a hurry to change it.

Read this card

3. The Tricks of the Mind

The closer you come to silence, the subtler the mind’s tricks can become: chasing experiences, naming silence, or announcing “I have arrived.”

Reading

Do Not Be Deceived by Experiences

Anything that can be experienced—pain, pleasure, silence, energy, emptiness—is still content within consciousness. The book turns attention back toward the experiencer.

Read this card
Reading

The Mind Re-enters and Deceives

One of the mind's subtlest tricks is to label silence the moment it appears and announce, 'I have arrived.' In this way it enters again through another door.

Only Witnessing Can Truly Dance
