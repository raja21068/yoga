Three Basic Points

Whatever the outer form of a method, the book reduces the shared core to three things: relaxation, witnessing, and non-judgment.

Read the Book3. Guidelines to Freedom

What this chapter is saying

First, do not fight the mind or suppress it through control and concentration. Second, watch whatever is happening with relaxed awareness. Third, do not immediately divide experience into good/bad or right/wrong.

Why it matters

The book gives many different methods but says these basic points run through them. The actions of the methods may even be opposite: some dance, some sit; some make sound, some are silent. These inner attitudes are what they share.

What it means for practice

This card can be used as a check for the whole website. Whether you are doing an active or passive meditation, ask: is the body a little more relaxed? Am I watching? Am I busy judging everything that happens?

Three-Point Check
  1. Relaxation: am I fighting myself, or can I allow this moment to be as it is for now?
  2. Witnessing: do I know what is happening now?
  3. Non-judgment: can I see clearly before deciding whether anything needs to change?
