The Right Method Will Click

Play with a method for at least three days. If it feels intimate and naturally right for you, then give it your attention and go deeper.

Read the Book1. Methods and Meditation

What this chapter is saying

The book does not ask everyone to use the same method. It suggests trying methods and allowing your body and inner sense to reveal whether one 'clicks.' This fit is not a theoretical judgment; it appears through actual experience.

The rhythm given in the book

First play with a method for at least three days. If there is a sense of closeness and natural attraction, stop constantly switching and follow the method seriously for a longer period. The book even suggests at least three months as a direction for deeper work.

What it means for practice

This is the basis of the website's three-day experiment. The three days are not for scoring the method, but for giving it a real chance to happen. Afterward you can decide whether to continue, pause, or try something else.

3-Day Experiment
  1. Day 1: learn the stages; do not judge.
  2. Day 2: compare less and follow the method completely.
  3. Day 3: only after the practice, notice whether the method is making you more joyful, sensitive, and clear.
