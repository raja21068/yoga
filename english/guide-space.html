Enough Space

In the beginning, genuinely set aside a period of time in which meditation will not be interrupted.

Read the Book2. Advice for Beginners

What this chapter is saying

The book gives very practical advice: temporarily disconnect the phone and daily business, leave concerns outside, and let this period stop serving work, desire, planning, and everyday busyness.

The action in the book

Taking off your shoes in this section is more than a physical act; it becomes a small ritual. With the shoes, you also leave outside concerns for a while. You know you are entering a different way of using time.

What it means for practice

The point is not that the session must be one hour. The point is to give the practice a complete boundary. Five minutes repeatedly interrupted by messages is very different from twenty minutes in which you are truly unavailable.

Before You Begin
  1. Put the phone on Do Not Disturb or airplane mode.
  2. Tell people around you that you do not want to be interrupted during this period.
  3. Before beginning, write down anything still pulling at you and tell yourself you will return to it afterward.
