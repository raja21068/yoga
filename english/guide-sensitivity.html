The Growth of Sensitivity

Meditation does not make a person numb and withdrawn; it makes the capacity to feel life finer and richer.

Read the Book2. Meditation in Flowering

What this chapter is saying

The book presents sensitivity as part of the flowering of meditation. As awareness becomes finer, you not only understand yourself more clearly; you also feel more deeply that you belong to this world rather than standing outside it.

The image in the book

The book specifically points to a blade of grass: being small does not make it unimportant; it has its own uniqueness. As sensitivity grows, friendship with trees, birds, mountains, rivers, oceans, and stars becomes possible again.

What it means for practice

Sensitivity here does not mean being easily hurt. It means perception is no longer coarse. The meditator begins to notice sounds, touch, breath, light, and subtle changes in relationship that were previously missed.

Try This Today
  1. Choose something small you normally overlook: a leaf, a breeze, a distant sound.
  2. Do not analyze it; give it thirty seconds of complete attention.
  3. See whether 'seeing more finely' also brings you closer to this moment.
