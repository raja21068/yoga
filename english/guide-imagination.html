Imagination Can Help

The book treats imagination as a real psychological capacity. It can be part of a method, but it must not be confused with ultimate reality.

Read the Book1. Methods and Meditation

What this chapter is saying

This section does not dismiss imagination as 'false.' The book emphasizes that imagination is a capacity that can influence body and mind, and some meditation methods consciously use this capacity.

The example in the book

The book refers to Tibetan heat yoga to show that sustained imagination can be accompanied by bodily responses. The point is not to copy that practice, but to show that imagination is treated here as a usable form of energy.

What it means for practice

At the same time, the book warns that what imagination creates must not be mistaken for the end. A method may use imagery to help you enter a state, but finally you return to awareness and distinguish 'what I imagined' from 'what I directly saw.'

Principle for Use
  1. If a method specifically asks for visualization, use imagination as instructed.
  2. If a method does not ask for it, do not add images just to chase a special experience.
  3. Whatever imagery appears, treat it as content that can be witnessed.
