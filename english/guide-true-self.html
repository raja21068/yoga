Your True Self

Meditation is not creating a better 'me'; it helps you discover the nature hidden beneath habit, role, and social expectation.

Read the Book2. Meditation in Flowering

What this chapter is saying

The book emphasizes that the true self is not something you manufacture later; it is already there. The problem is that people often mistake social roles, identities, and ideas of who they 'should be' for themselves.

The concept in the book

The book points to the root of the word personality, persona—a mask. The point is not to reject all social roles, but not to mistake the mask for the whole of who you are.

What it means for practice

When borrowed answers and outside direction become quieter, intuition has room to appear. The book treats individuality as unique: not copying an ideal personality, but discovering what truly allows your life to flower.

One Question
  1. When a choice appears today, separate two voices: what do others expect me to do, and what do I actually see inside?
  2. There is no need to rebel against anyone immediately; first learn to distinguish the voices.
