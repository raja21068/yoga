These Methods Are Simple

The simpler a method is, the harder the mind may find it to trust, because ego prefers difficulties it can conquer.

Read the Book1. Methods and Meditation

What this chapter is saying

The book reminds us that complexity, difficulty, and mystery easily make the ego think, 'This is a worthy challenge.' A simple method can look too ordinary, and so people miss it.

The observation in the book

Ego likes peaks, obstacles, and victory because overcoming difficulty reinforces the feeling 'I did it.' Simple methods offer less to boast about, so they do not feed ego in the same way.

What it means for practice

Do not add layers to a method in order to make it seem advanced. One breath, one period of stillness, one act of seeing—if that is how the book gives it, first let it remain simple.

Today’s Reminder
  1. When a method seems 'too simple,' do not dismiss it immediately.
  2. Ask whether it truly does not suit you, or whether the mind simply finds it insufficiently complex and challenging.
