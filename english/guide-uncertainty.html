Tune In to Uncertainty

When the old ego begins to loosen, familiar rules for living also stop working. The book treats this strangeness and uncertainty as an important transition in the inner journey.

Read the Book8. Tune In to Uncertainty

What is the question?

The questioner sees more and more of the false nature of ego through witnessing. Old standards of life are disappearing, he no longer knows what is real, and he feels anxious.

The turn in the answer

The book says this is a good sign: the old master is disappearing while a deeper order has not yet become visible. Chaos naturally lies between. The danger is not uncertainty itself, but becoming frightened and running back to the old false certainty.

The three stages in the book

A Zen image says: at first mountains are mountains and rivers are rivers; in meditation mountains are no longer mountains and rivers no longer rivers; finally mountains are mountains again and rivers are rivers. The third stage is not a return to old certainty, but a larger stability that can include uncertainty.

Learn to Continue Without the Old Map
  1. When uncertainty appears, do not rush to manufacture a new identity.
  2. Let anxiety itself be witnessed without allowing it to decide your direction.
  3. Remember: when old certainty disappears, reality itself has not disappeared.
