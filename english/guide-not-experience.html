You Are Not Your Experiences

Silence, bliss, emptiness, light, or energy can all be experienced. Whatever can be seen is not the final 'you.'

Read the Book3. Guidelines to Freedom

What this chapter is saying

The book prevents the meditator from stopping at beautiful inner experiences. An experience can be deep, exquisite, or ecstatic, but as long as 'I am experiencing it,' it remains an object being seen.

The image in the book

The inner journey is like passing through many landscapes—trees, mountains, flowers, rivers, sun, moon. Enjoy the scenery, but do not mistake a beautiful view for home.

What it means for practice

For this reason the website does not turn 'seeing light,' 'feeling energy,' or 'becoming very silent' into levels or achievement badges. Such things may happen or may not. The direction remains witnessing.

When a Special Experience Appears
  1. Allow the experience to happen fully.
  2. Do not cling to it or hurry to reproduce it.
  3. Remember only: this experience too is being seen.
